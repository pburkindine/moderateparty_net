## Why

The FB advertising block is probably a *political-ads authorization* problem,
not a true ban: Meta requires ID-verified advertiser authorization + "Paid for by"
disclaimers for social-issue/political ads, and most moderate-politics accounts
stuck at "blocked" are stuck there. One honest appeal cycle is cheap. But the
strategic lesson of the block stands: never again depend on a platform's mercy
(see MP-202).

## What

1. Diagnose the actual state: ad account status, page status, any policy strikes.
2. Complete Meta's **political ads authorization**: ID verification, confirmed
   page ownership, "Paid for by" disclaimer setup.
3. If genuinely banned: one appeal with documentation, then stop. Do not grind.
4. Write down the outcome here either way, so future-us doesn't re-litigate it.

- [ ] Root cause identified and recorded in this file
- [ ] Authorization completed OR appeal filed and resolved
