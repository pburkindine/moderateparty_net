# MP-205 — Be linkable when the Ranked Choice Voting wave comes looking

- **ID**: MP-205
- **Track**: 2 — Distribution
- **Status**: todo
- **Priority**: P1 — the one structural plank, and it's a live national issue
- **Depends-on**: MP-101 (a modern RCV page worth linking)

## Why

RCV is the site's load-bearing structural plank — the site itself admits the
moderate party "would just die off as a spoiler party" without it — and it's
been early on this since ~2005, back when it was crankery and before Maine and
Alaska enacted it. Organizations in this space (FairVote et al.) need exactly
what this site has: likable, funny, disarming explanations. This story is NOT
a partnership (no trust required) — it's about being *findable and linkable*
when they go looking.

## What

1. Upgrade ranked-choice.html into the best friendly RCV explainer on the
   internet: the existing voice + `facts/ranked-choice-voting.md` citations +
   MP-102 shareable card. This is the flagship page of the whole program.
2. SEO-target "ranked choice voting explained" and kin (with MP-103).
3. Light-touch visibility: make sure RCV orgs and educators *can* find it —
   a couple of polite pointers, resource-list submissions where they exist.
   No partnership asks, no keys, no trust extended.
4. When RCV news breaks (ballot measures, elections using it), the X queue
   (MP-203) has the explainer ready to ride the moment.
