- **ID**: MP-202

- **Depends-on**: nothing (better after MP-101 gives it something to point at)

## Why

Email is the only distribution channel no platform can revoke. It converts
drive-by visitors into a durable audience that survives any ban, algorithm
change, or platform collapse. For a project scared of depending on others,
this is the safest possible move.

## What

1. Pick a provider that fits the ethos: cheap/free tier, easy export (the list
   is Pete's, portable forever), no tracking creepiness. Buttondown or similar
   indie-friendly options; evaluate and record the choice here.
2. **Signup on the site** in the site's voice — an invitation, not a popup
   ambush. This site wants nothing from its visitors; the signup should feel
   like joining, not being captured.
3. Cadence: occasional "Letters from the moderate Party" dispatch — new planks,
   a good meme, an RCV development. No content treadmill; when there's something
   to say.
4. First letter drafted and sent to the founding subscribers (even if that's
   Pete + Claude + gramma).
